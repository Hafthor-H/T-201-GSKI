The message from Claude talks about how people are outsourcing there thinking to a generative AI.

It compares how people scroll reels like this one for hours and then when it comes to doing actual

work they just get an AI to do it for them. I think this is a bad development all around since the 

problem solving skills don't grow while using AI, at least not as much, and what will you do when you

encounter a problem the AI can't solve.

While I sometimes use AI as an enhanced  search engine, I am very against using it in my studies 

particularly for this reason. I want to struggle, I want to bang my head against the table. Because what

will we be if we outsource all our thinking.



Also I think the part about "tolerance for difficulty" was a great line that describes the issue with AI

in studies. Especially undergrad where problems are easier for the AI to solve.
